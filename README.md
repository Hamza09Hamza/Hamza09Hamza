## Featured projects

### [Doctor Assistant](https://github.com/Hamza09Hamza/doctor_assistant)

An experimental medical-imaging research platform that connects specialized models through a structured analysis pipeline. It explores modality-aware routing, structured findings, grounded report generation, verification, recommendations, and auditable outputs.

`Python` · `PyTorch` · `Medical Imaging` · `Model Orchestration` · `Grounded Reporting`

> Research prototype only; not clinically validated or intended for patient care.

### [Latent-Djezzy](https://github.com/Hamza09Hamza/Latent-Djezzy)

A multilingual analytics assistant supporting French, English, Arabic, and Algerian Darija. It combines RAG, schema-aware text-to-SQL, deterministic KPI formatting, charts, reports, voice interaction, and API/WebSocket access.

`Python` · `LangGraph` · `RAG` · `SQL` · `FastAPI` · `WebSockets`

### [Labo-IBS](https://github.com/Hamza09Hamza/Labo-IBS)

A laboratory integration bridge that receives analyzer results over TCP, handles machine-specific protocols and decoding rules, maps test codes, stores normalized results in PostgreSQL, and provides administration tools.

`Python` · `Flask` · `PostgreSQL` · `ASTM` · `HL7 MLLP` · `TCP Integration`

### [Client-Patient](https://github.com/Hamza09Hamza/Client-Patient)

A bilingual patient portal for securely viewing laboratory-report PDFs delivered by a clinic system. It includes patient provisioning, a protected integration API, a custom PDF viewer, QR-based single-report access, audit logging, and production deployment tooling.

`Next.js` · `TypeScript` · `PostgreSQL` · `Prisma` · `Tailwind CSS` · `Docker`

### [WhatsApp-like Communication Server](https://github.com/Hamza09Hamza/whatsapp-server)

A real-time messaging and calling backend with private and group chat, delivery/read receipts, call signaling, Mediasoup SFU audio/video, PostgreSQL persistence, and server-side recording.

`Node.js` · `Express` · `Socket.IO` · `Mediasoup` · `PostgreSQL` · `FFmpeg`

### [Forest Fire Prediction](https://github.com/Hamza09Hamza/ForestFirePrediction)

A data-mining and machine-learning pipeline for forest-fire prediction in Algeria and Tunisia using satellite fire detections, climate, soil, and elevation data. Includes preprocessing, supervised learning, clustering, and from-scratch algorithm implementations.

`Python` · `Pandas` · `scikit-learn` · `GeoPandas` · `Machine Learning` · `Data Mining`
